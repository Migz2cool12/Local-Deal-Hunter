Privacy Policy

Local Deal Hunter is a prototype. This version does not require an account and stores saved deals only on your device.

Location

If you choose to use location, your browser may provide your approximate device location to the app so nearby results can be shown. This prototype does not send your location to a server.

Data

The prototype does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, payment information, or precise location on a server.

Changes

This policy will be updated before live retailer data, accounts, analytics, advertising, or other third-party services are added.
